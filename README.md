## 🌌 Overview

**Techfest 2050** is a futuristic digital reimagination of **Techfest, IIT Bombay**, envisioning how Asia's premier science and technology festival could evolve by the year 2050.

The project combines immersive storytelling, next-generation user experience design, futuristic visual aesthetics, and interactive engagement to create a compelling vision of the future of technological events.

Rather than simply redesigning a website, Techfest 2050 presents a complete digital experience where innovation, artificial intelligence, immersive interfaces, and human creativity converge.
